Dolar+

Native iOS product for Argentine exchange rates, built for clarity, speed, and reliable daily use.

Dolar+ is current proof of native iOS execution: built with SwiftUI, modular architecture, real-time exchange rate integrations, widgets, and a privacy-conscious product approach designed for everyday decision-making.

The Problem

In Argentina, accessing reliable and updated dollar exchange rates often requires navigating multiple sources, manually comparing values, and dealing with cluttered interfaces.

Information is fragmented across different exchange rate types.

Interfaces prioritize ads or data volume over clarity.

There is a lack of tools designed from the perspective of someone who checks exchange rates daily.

Value Proposition

Dolar+ was designed as a focused native product: fast to read, technically maintainable, and deliberately low-noise.

Focused core flow

The product turns a repetitive financial check into a fast, readable experience instead of a cluttered market dashboard.

Native ecosystem fit

Widgets and Apple ecosystem integrations make exchange rates available in the right context, not only inside the app.

Sustainable technical base

The architecture favors modular boundaries, clear data flows, and decisions that support iteration without unnecessary complexity.

Interface and Experience

Architecture and Technical Scope

Dolar+ is built as a real production iOS product, with attention to maintainability, performance, release quality, and ecosystem fit.

  • Native iOS application built with SwiftUI.
  • Modular architecture designed for maintainability, clear boundaries, and testability.
  • Integration with real-time exchange rate APIs and data normalization.
  • Widgets and Apple ecosystem integrations for fast, glanceable access.
  • Product decisions shaped to stay focused, low-noise, and privacy-conscious.
